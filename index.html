This isn't a test. There are no right answers. It's just a way to figure out what actually makes you tick — so your high school years can be about something you genuinely care about.

This works best when you answer as yourself — not the version of yourself you think you should be, and not who your parents or teachers want you to be.

There are no right or wrong answers here. Someone who loves gaming is just as interesting as someone who loves building robots. Someone who wants a simple, happy life is just as valid as someone who wants to change the world.

Just be honest. It works better that way.
